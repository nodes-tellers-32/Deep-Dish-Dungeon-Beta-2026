## 🕹️ About

Deep Dish Dungeon is a survival exploration dungeon crawler developed by Behold Studios and published by Raw Fury. Explore a hand-crafted, puzzle-filled dungeon solo or with up to two friends, gathering resources, crafting tools, cooking meals, and uncovering its hidden mysteries. Join our beta testing program, help shape the experience before its October 13, 2026 release, and receive a free game key.

Deep Dish Dungeon is for players who enjoy atmospheric dungeon crawlers, exploration, puzzles, crafting, survival systems, and cooperative adventures.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Hand-Crafted Dungeon** — Explore atmospheric corridors, rooms, passageways, and hidden secrets.
- 🧩 **Puzzle-Driven Exploration** — Solve environmental puzzles and investigate every shadow to progress.
- 🛠️ **Crafting and Resource Gathering** — Scavenge materials and create tools that help you reach deeper areas.
- 🍲 **Cooking for Progress** — Prepare meals with unique effects to strengthen your expedition.
- 🤝 **Online Co-op** — Team up with up to two friends to explore, survive, and share discoveries.
- 👹 **Unpredictable Enemies** — Learn enemy behaviors and adapt your approach to survive the dungeon.

**A:** Simply sign in with your Steam account, browse available beta programs, request access to games you're interested in, and start testing. After you complete the beta, you'll receive a free Steam key for the full game upon its official release.

**Q: Do I need to pay to join?**
**A:** No, joining a beta test is completely free. All we ask for is your time and genuine feedback.

**Q: Is my Steam account safe?**

**Q: When will I receive the Steam key?**
**A:** Keys are sent via email once the game you tested officially releases on Steam. We'll notify you at the email address associated with your Steam account.

**Q: Will I get access right away?**
**A:** After you sign in with Steam, your tester profile needs to be approved. Approvals go out as spots free up — roughly once a week. Once approved, beta tests become visible and requestable.

**Q: What is the reward for testing?**
